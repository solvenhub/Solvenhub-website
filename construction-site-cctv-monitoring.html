How does remote monitoring work for construction sites?

SolvenHub connects to your site’s cameras — including temporary site setups — and trained operators watch them live during your off-hours, detecting unauthorized activity and responding according to the escalation rules you define.

What parts of a job site can be monitored?

Equipment yards, material staging areas, site gates and entrances, fencing and perimeters — coverage follows your site plan and current phase.

Do you cover nights, weekends, and holidays?

Yes. Off-hours coverage is the core of this service — nights, weekends, holidays, and project shutdown periods, set around your schedule.

How do alerts and escalation work?

When something suspicious happens, our team notifies your designated contacts right away — and, based on your approved escalation rules, can escalate incidents to local authorities where appropriate.

What reports will I receive?

Morning summaries with screenshots, incident reports with timestamps and evidence clips, and activity logs — ready for your site meeting.

How do we get started?

We start with a free security assessment, review your site plan and cameras, define monitoring and escalation rules, securely connect your feeds, then begin monitoring and reporting.
