The problem

After hours is when gas stations are most exposed

Forecourt incidents, loitering, and break-ins don’t wait for opening time — and recording-only cameras only help after the damage is done.

Forecourt & night activity

Suspicious vehicles, loitering, and after-hours movement around pumps and the lot go unnoticed when nobody is watching live.

Cash-sensitive areas

Registers, safes, and back-office areas need consistent oversight — especially during late-night and early-morning shifts.

Unauthorized access

Back doors, storage rooms, and restricted areas can be entered without anyone knowing until the next morning.

Footage reviewed too late

Most stations only review recordings after an incident. By then, the opportunity to respond has already passed.

How does remote CCTV monitoring work for gas stations?

SolvenHub connects to your station’s existing camera feeds and trained operators watch them live around the clock, identifying suspicious activity and responding according to the escalation rules you define.

What areas of my station can be monitored?

Forecourt and fuel pumps, entrances, cash registers and cash-sensitive zones, back rooms and storage, parking areas, and lot perimeters — coverage is set around your layout and priorities.

How do alerts and escalation work?

When something suspicious happens, our team notifies your designated contacts right away — and, based on your approved escalation rules, can escalate incidents to local authorities where appropriate.

What reports will I receive?

You can receive hourly updates with screenshots, clear incident reports with timestamps and evidence clips, and Excel activity sheets documenting monitored activity.

Do I need to replace my existing cameras?

No. We work with your existing CCTV infrastructure. During onboarding we review your camera setup and flag offline cameras, blind areas, or tampering.

How do we get started?

We start with a free security assessment, review your station layout and cameras, define monitoring and escalation rules, securely connect your feeds, then begin monitoring and reporting. Reach out on WhatsApp or book an assessment to begin.
