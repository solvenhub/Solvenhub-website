How it works

How remote monitoring works

Five steps, the same for a single Florida store or several locations.

1. Camera review and setup

We review your existing camera setup first and confirm what can be monitored. We may be able to monitor your existing setup — we would need to review the system first.

2. Monitoring rules

You approve the rules in advance: what operators watch for, who your designated contacts are, and how incidents should be escalated.

3. Live monitoring

Trained operators watch your live feeds around the clock — not recordings reviewed after something has already happened.

4. Alerts and escalation

When suspicious activity is detected, operators alert your designated contacts and, where appropriate, escalate incidents to local authorities based on your approved escalation rules.

5. Reporting and documentation

Incidents are documented, and monitoring activity is reported to you as set out in your plan, so you keep a clear record.

Does SolvenHub provide CCTV monitoring across Florida?

Yes. SolvenHub delivers remote CCTV monitoring to businesses across Florida, and monitoring is available nationwide across all 50 U.S. states. Current Florida clients include Plantation Smoke Shop in Plantation and V&V in Miramar, alongside other Florida smoke shops.

Can you monitor my existing cameras?

We may be able to monitor your existing setup. We would need to review the system first. During a free security assessment, we review your current cameras and confirm what monitoring options may be available.

Do I need to replace my CCTV system?

Not always. Whether your current system can be monitored depends on the review of your setup. If something cannot be monitored reliably, we will explain the options before you decide anything.

Can you monitor overnight?

Yes. Trained operators monitor live around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays — not just during business hours.

How do alerts and escalation work?

When an operator sees suspicious activity, they follow the escalation rules you approved in advance: alerting your designated contacts and, where appropriate, escalating incidents to local authorities based on your approved escalation rules.

Do you provide incident reports?

Yes. Incidents our operators handle are documented, and monitoring activity is reported to you as set out in your plan, so you have a record of what was seen and what action was taken.

Can you monitor multiple Florida locations?

Yes. Each location is set up with its own monitoring rules and designated contacts, and locations can be managed together under one service relationship.

Is SolvenHub physically located in Florida?

No. SolvenHub is based in India and delivers monitoring remotely. There is no SolvenHub office in Florida — your cameras are watched live by our remote monitoring team, and alerts follow your approved escalation rules.
