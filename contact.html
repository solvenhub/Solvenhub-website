FAQ

Before you message

Quick answers so you know what to expect when you reach out.

How fast will I get a response?

WhatsApp is monitored 24/7, so inquiries there get the fastest response. Calls and emails are answered by the team during working hours.

How does onboarding work?

We start with a short discovery call, review your camera setup and locations, agree on monitoring and escalation rules, then securely connect the feeds and begin monitoring with reporting.

What information do you need from me?

Your business name and locations, the camera system you use, how many cameras and sites you want covered, and what kind of reporting you want (hourly screenshots, Excel sheets, alerts).

Do you work outside the United States?

Our clients are US-based businesses. If you operate in the US, reach out and we will talk through your setup.
