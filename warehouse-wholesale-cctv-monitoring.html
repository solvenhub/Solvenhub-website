How does remote monitoring work for a warehouse?

SolvenHub connects to your facility cameras and trained operators watch them live around the clock, detecting unauthorized activity and responding according to the escalation rules you define.

What areas of our facility can be covered?

Loading docks, yards and trailer parking, gates and entry lanes, high-value inventory zones, perimeter fencing, and receiving offices — coverage is prioritized by zone and risk.

Do you cover nights, weekends, and shutdown periods?

Yes. Off-hours coverage is the core of this service — nights, weekends, holidays, and shutdown periods, set around your operating schedule.

How do alerts and escalation work?

When something suspicious happens, our team notifies your designated contacts right away — and, based on your approved escalation rules, can escalate incidents to local authorities where appropriate.

What reports and evidence will we receive?

You can receive hourly updates with screenshots, incident reports with timestamps and evidence clips useful for insurance and carrier disputes, and activity sheets documenting monitored activity.

Do we need to replace our existing cameras? How do we get started?

No. We work with your existing CCTV and NVR infrastructure. We start with a free security assessment, review your facility and cameras, define monitoring and escalation rules, securely connect your feeds, then begin monitoring and reporting.
