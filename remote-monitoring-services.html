What does SolvenHub monitor?

Our trained surveillance team watches your live CCTV feeds around the clock. We identify unusual behavior and respond according to the security protocols you define — turning recording-only cameras into a live protection system.

How do alerts and escalation work?

When something suspicious happens, we immediately notify your security personnel, your internal team, or local law enforcement — based on your rulebook. No delays, no missed incidents.

What reports will I receive?

You can receive hourly updates with screenshots, clear incident reports with timestamps and evidence clips, and Excel activity sheets documenting monitored activity — reporting is customized around your operation's needs.

Are plans customizable?

Yes. Every plan is customized around your locations, camera coverage, monitoring hours, and reporting needs. Message us on WhatsApp for a tailored quote — no fixed pricing, because no two businesses are alike.

Do I need to replace my existing cameras?

No. We work with your existing CCTV infrastructure. During onboarding we review your camera setup and flag offline cameras, low-light or blind areas, tampering, and network issues so your system stays reliable.

How do we get started?
